# Portable rules — a warning (for humans)

These rules come from the style of their author. They are not metaphysics: they
are opinions, from various experiences, and they change often. A copy is a
snapshot, not a law.

Take what serves the project, adapt what does not, and delete what does not
fit. A rule is a trace of a practice, not a truth. If a rule conflicts with the
project's own needs, the project wins.

They carry no guarantee and no universal claim. What travels here is a way of
working, not a standard imposed on everyone.
